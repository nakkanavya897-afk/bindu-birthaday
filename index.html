Dear Bindluuuu… 💌🎀💗

Happy Birthday to one of the most precious people my childhood gave me.

When I think about the past 15 years, I don't just remember school days. I remember a version of us that was completely carefree — laughing over silly things, talking about people, making each other laugh and turning ordinary days into memories.

We grew up together in a way. We watched each other change, make mistakes, laugh, get annoyed, dream, and become the people we are today. And that is what makes our friendship so special to me.

You are not just someone I have known for 15 years. You are a part of my childhood, a part of my happiest memories, and a person whose name will always have a special corner in my heart.

There may be new people, new places and new chapters in both our lives, but I hope one thing never changes — the little “us” that can still laugh at absolutely anything.

And Bindluuuu, there are a few things I really want you to know. Your care, your love, and the way you protect the people you love mean more to me than I can ever explain. 🎀🤍 You have treated me with a kind of warmth that makes me feel like I am not just your friend, but a part of your family. 🫂💗

Please always treat me like I am one of your own family, because that is exactly how special you are to me. 🥹❤️ I want us to be there for each other through every situation — the happy ones, the difficult ones, the silly ones and all the moments in between. ⭐🫶

Be with me always, till my last breath. ♾️💋 Don't forget me in any situation, no matter how busy life becomes or how many new chapters come our way. I want to be the person you can always come back to, and I want you to know that you are that person for me too. 💕

And yes… in my heart, you will always be my little Bindluuuu, my baby, my family and one of the most precious people in my life. 🎀👶🏻💗

On your birthday, I wish you a life full of happiness, peaceful days, big dreams coming true and people who love you exactly the way you deserve.

And whenever life gets difficult, I hope you remember that somewhere there will always be your Navya, cheering for you, laughing with you and annoying you. 😌😂

Happy Birthday, my Bindluuuu. 🥹💗💋🎀⭐
Thank you for being one of the most beautiful chapters of my childhood — and I hope you stay in the rest of my story too.

Forever your Navya 💕🎀♾️
